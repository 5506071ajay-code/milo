What MILO does

Built for how student money actually moves.

Zero-manual-entry journal

Every transaction from connected banks and cards lands in one journal, categorised, with internal transfers recognised and never counted as spending. Cash and informal loans are the only things you type.

AI CFO, grounded

Ask "can I afford ₹6,000 for a course this month?" and get an answer computed from your commitments, reservations and forecast, with every step of the reasoning shown. It never invents a number.

Split-Bill Memory with verified people

Equal, unequal and partial splits between real MILO accounts, not typed names. Balances, reminders and settlements follow the person, and the fewest-payments engine clears a group trip in one pass.

Purpose-based money

₹30,000 from home is hostel, food, transport, education, emergency and flexible money before it is a balance. Bank balance is not spendable balance, and MILO keeps the difference visible.

Forecast & Money Calendar

Committed versus flexible, irregular-income modelling, and a calendar that projects your balance through rent, EMIs and SIPs so the lowest point is visible before it happens.

Peer emergency liquidity

Short-term help from people who know you, with a reason, a date and no interest. Requests reach only the verified people you have split with or your college network.

Allowance Survival Mode

A month becomes a daily number, recalculated when today goes over.

"Bro, where did my money go?"

Category totals, the comparison that explains them, and why spending rose.

The ₹100 Problem

34 small purchases are still ₹3,920. See them, and what cutting 25% saves.

Student Money Health

Consent, not credentials

How your data gets in, and what never does.

1Sign in with Google. That identity is your MILO account. There is no password to create.
2Pick a provider. MILO lists exactly which scopes it will read: balances and transactions, nothing more.
3Approve on the provider's own screen. Banks via the RBI Account Aggregator framework; brokers via Zerodha Kite or Upstox OAuth. MILO never sees what you type there.
4Synced, timestamped, revocable. Every account shows when it was last synced; disconnecting revokes the consent and deletes the data.
What MILO never asks for
  • Net-banking passwords, OTPs, PINs, card CVVs
  • Access to accounts you did not explicitly approve
  • Permission to move money — MILO is read-only
  • More than balances and transactions
No fake numbers, ever. If a bank or broker cannot be connected through an authorised provider, MILO says so. The only synthetic data is the optional demo sandbox, and it is labelled DEMO on every screen.
Security

Treated like a financial application, because it is one.

Google OAuth only

Server-side authorization-code flow, verified ID tokens, signed httpOnly session cookies, revocable sessions. On Android, sign-in runs in the system browser and returns through a single-use deep-link code.

Encrypted tokens, least privilege

Provider tokens are AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest and deleted on disconnect. Every query is scoped to the signed-in user; nobody can read another person's accounts, splits or requests.

Auditable

Consent grants, syncs, disconnects, splits, payments, reminders and sign-ins are written to an audit trail you can see under Everything → Data & privacy.

Get MILO

One app, any MILO server.

Android app

Install MILO.apk (Android 5.1 and up, 3 MB). On first launch, enter your college or team's MILO server address once, then continue with Google. The APK is signed and works with every MILO deployment, so one file can be shared with everyone.

  1. Allow installs from your browser or file manager
  2. Open MILO → enter the server address (e.g. https://milo.yourcollege.edu)
  3. Continue with Google

Web app & self-hosting

The same product runs in any browser at your server's address. Hosting MILO takes one container and one Google OAuth client; Render, Fly.io and plain Docker configurations are included in the source.

docker build -t milo . && docker run -p 8787:8787 -v milo-data:/app/data --env-file .env milo

Bank connections need RBI Account Aggregator credentials (e.g. Setu); demat connections need a Zerodha Kite Connect or Upstox developer app. Until they are configured, MILO shows those providers as unavailable rather than pretending.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is MILO a bank or a wallet?

No. MILO is a read-only layer above the accounts you already have. It cannot move money.

Why can I only add people who have MILO?

Because a balance has to point at a real, verified person. Typed names drift, duplicate and forget. Invite someone by email; once they sign in with Google you can add them.

Is the Student Money Health score a credit score?

No. It is a 0–100 reflection of your own habits, computed only from your data, visible only to you. No bank sees it.

What happens when I disconnect an account?

MILO revokes the provider consent, deletes the stored token, and removes that connection's accounts and transactions from your identity.

Why does the Android app ask for a server address?

Your data lives on a MILO server run by your college or team, never on someone else's. The app is the same for everyone; only the address differs.
